# Use of AI

In line with the NASA Space Apps Challenge rules, we declare the use of AI in this project.

| Item | AI involvement |
|---|---|
| **Source code** (`app/feel_the_planet.py`) | Written by the team with the assistance of an AI coding assistant (Anthropic Claude). The team defined the requirements, tested on the real hardware and made the design decisions. |
| **Documentation** (README and `docs/`) | Drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by the team. |
| **Block diagram and NASA layer previews** (`docs/images/`) | Generated with Python scripts written with AI assistance. The NASA layer images are the original NASA GIBS imagery with added titles and legends. They are not AI-generated imagery. |
| **Voice audio** | Generated at runtime by Microsoft neural text-to-speech voices (edge-tts, `en-US-JennyNeural` and `es-MX-DaliaNeural`), or by the Windows built-in voice. This audio is AI-generated speech. |
| **Instrument sounds** | Synthesized mathematically by our code (additive synthesis). No AI audio generation is used. |
| **NASA data** | Unmodified NASA GIBS layers. No AI was used to create or alter scientific data. |

No NASA logos, flags or mission identifiers are used or modified.
